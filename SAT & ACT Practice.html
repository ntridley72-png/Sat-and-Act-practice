Frequently asked questions

Is FunSAT an official SAT or ACT website?

No. Questions are original practice items modeled on published skills, and all score estimates are unofficial.

How do score estimates work?

SAT estimates use the selected published practice-test curve. ACT estimates use the chart from ACT's free practice test and improve as you complete more scored practice.

Do arcade games change my estimated score?

No. Arcade and topic-practice activity never replaces scored SAT or ACT practice and does not directly change your estimate.
