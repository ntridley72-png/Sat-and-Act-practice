## Copy you can paste (edit to your own voice)

**One-line description (directories):**
Free Digital SAT and ACT practice in the browser: full practice tests, skill drills with explanations, typed-answer math,
score estimates, and college data from U.S. Department of Education sources. No account needed.

**Short description (100 words):**
FunSAT is a free SAT and ACT practice site that runs in any browser, including school Chromebooks. You can take a full
Digital SAT-style practice test, drill one skill at a time (circles, transitions, boundaries and 25 more), and see why
each wrong answer is wrong. Math includes typed answers with the real grid rules and a built-in Desmos calculator. The
college pages use federal data with the source and year on every number, and show admissions by year from Fall 2021 to
the latest release. No account is needed; progress stays on your device. FunSAT is independent and not affiliated with
the College Board or ACT.

**r/SAT resources-thread comment (only where the rules allow it):**
I made a free practice site, funsat.bid, mostly because I wanted skill drills without an account. Each SAT skill has a
page with worked examples and a drill, and math has typed answers that follow the grid rules. It is not official
material and not affiliated with the College Board. If a question is wrong, the Corrections page says how to report it
and I will fix it.

## Shareable features already in the product

- Skill pages: /sat-math/circles/ and the other 27 can be linked directly to a classmate who is stuck on one topic.
- College pages: each has a stable URL and the year-by-year table, good for "look at this school's trend".
- Score pages: /sat-scores/1300/ lists colleges whose middle 50% includes that score.
