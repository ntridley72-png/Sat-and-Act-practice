# Question variety and accuracy review — October 2, 2026

81 individually authored questions added: 36 SAT math, 36 SAT reading/writing, and 9 ACT science. They are original modeled practice, not released or endorsed College Board items.

Basis: College Board’s [Reading and Writing specification](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test/reading-writing) and [Math domains](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test/math/types). SAT reading/writing items use one question per short text or text pair and cover all four domains. New text lengths are checked at 25–150 words. SAT math covers the four published domains. ACT science is kept out of SAT selection.

Three passes:

1. Independently solved the 36 math problems, checked substitutions, units, excluded radical roots, conditional-probability denominators, squared area scaling, and the distinction between a quadratic maximum’s time and value. Alternate computations are recorded in tests/variety.test.cjs. Read the 36 English items against their texts and grammar rules; checked the 9 science items against the supplied data and experimental conditions.
2. Checked all four options for a unique best answer and the supplied explanations for consistency. Reading answers must follow only from the supplied text; wrong choices contradict it, omit the stated rhetorical goal, or claim information it does not establish. Grammar checks cover clause boundaries, singular subjects, modifiers, tense, pronouns, possessives, and parallel structure. The factual contexts are self-contained practice scenarios.
3. Checked SAT domain, text length, question structure, choice uniqueness, and current bank compatibility. Ran exact-ID repeat tests, family rotation tests, real-bank sampling tests, and account-history/Groq regressions.

Difficulty labels are author estimates, not empirically calibrated SAT difficulty. These questions supplement preparation; they do not reproduce the official test’s calibrated scoring.

Rotation now groups the older generated variants by template and groups shared passages together. Exact question IDs remain excluded for 50 selections whenever the pool permits it. Among eligible questions, different families are preferred, followed by skill variety, with domain balancing retained. A limited topic pool relaxes family diversity rather than refusing to start a drill. Family history persists in the account profile; existing recent IDs are migrated into family history.
