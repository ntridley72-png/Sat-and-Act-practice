College Admissions Chances: How to Read the Numbers

Most "chance calculators" are entertainment. Real planning comes from reading a few numbers correctly and building a balanced list.

The three numbers that matter

  • Admit rate: what share of applicants were admitted. Under 10% is unpredictable for everyone.
  • Middle 50% (25th–75th): test scores of enrolled students — not a cutoff, and not "admitted" students.
  • Net price: the average amount students actually pay after aid, which is what your family budget should be built on.

Safety, match, reach — without the myths

A school is a match when your scores sit inside its middle 50% and your GPA is typical. A safety is one where you are comfortably above the 75th percentile and admission is likely. A reach is not just "famous" — it is any school where your numbers are below the 25th percentile or the admit rate is very low.
