Free 4-Week Digital SAT Study Plan

Four weeks is enough to move a score if you practice deliberately. The plan below takes about 45–60 minutes a day, with one longer session each week.

Week 1 — baseline and habits

  • Take one full-length practice set timed, no notes.
  • Build an error log: question type, why you missed it, and the rule you will apply next time.
  • Drill the Desmos calculator: 15 minutes a day on intersections, zeros, and lists.

Week 2 — weak domains first

  • Two 25-question sets in your weakest domains.
  • Review every wrong answer until you can explain it out loud.
  • Reading habit: summarize each passage in one sentence before answering.

Week 3 — timing and full sections

  • Full Reading & Writing module, then full Math module, under real timing.
  • Practice Module 2 hard mode deliberately — the real test adapts.
  • Keep the error log; spot repeats.

Week 4 — simulate and taper

  • One full simulation in the morning, same as test day.
  • Light review only for the last two days: error log, formulas, calculator warm-up.
  • Sleep schedule, not cramming.
