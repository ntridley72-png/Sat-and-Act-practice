Frequently asked questions

Is ACT Science required?

Science is optional on the ACT and does not change the composite under ACT's current rule, but some colleges and scholarships still ask for it.

How is the ACT composite calculated?

It is the average of English, Math, and Reading, rounded to the nearest whole number, on a 1–36 scale.

Can I practice one ACT section at a time?

Yes — pick a single section or take all four in one sitting.
