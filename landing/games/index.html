Free Study-Break Games That Reward Studying

FunSAT's arcade has 28 browser games — racing, action, puzzle, word, and table games — with nothing to download. You unlock game time by answering SAT and ACT practice questions, so breaks stay tied to progress.

The games

  • Racing: Neon Racing, Drift Circuit, Dirt Bike Trails
  • Action: Arena Protocol, Star Defender, Crossy Hopper, Ledge Leaper, Breakout, Sky Runner
  • Puzzle: 2048, Line Drop, Water Sort, Minesweeper, Sudoku, Block Fit, Bubble Shooter, Stack Tower
  • Word & table: Vocab Sprint, Word Finder, Four in a Row, Fleet Hunter, Tic-Tac-Toe, Blackjack, Solitaire
  • Classics: Dot Muncher, Snake, Pong, and more

How credits work

Every 3 practice questions earns 1 arcade credit. Round-based games give a full run per credit; timed games add 30 seconds. Practice and games share one account and one save file.

Frequently asked questions

Do the games work on school Chromebooks?

Yes — everything runs in the browser with no install, and the games are lightweight.

How do I earn game time?

Every 3 answered practice questions earns 1 arcade credit; bonuses come from streaks and correct-answer combos.

Is there anything to download?

No. FunSAT is a web app; games load instantly in the browser.

Do games affect my score estimate?

No. Arcade play never changes practice scores or your estimate.
