Frequently asked questions

Is FunSAT's SAT practice really free?

Yes. Practice tests, score estimates, and the calculator are free. An account is optional and only adds cross-device saving.

Is this an official SAT practice test?

No. FunSAT provides original practice questions modeled on published SAT skills, and every score estimate is unofficial.

How accurate is the score estimate?

It uses a published practice-test curve and shows a range rather than a single number. It becomes more useful as you complete more scored sets.

Can I use the Desmos calculator?

Yes — the built-in graphing calculator is powered by Desmos, the same calculator available in the digital SAT app.
