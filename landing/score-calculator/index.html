Free SAT & ACT Score Calculators

Finish a practice set and FunSAT turns your raw results into an unofficial SAT or ACT estimate — shown as a range, with percentile context and a comparison against real college ranges.

How the estimates work

  • SAT: section results are mapped with a published practice-test curve to 200–800 per section and 400–1600 total.
  • ACT: section scores from ACT's free practice-test chart; composite averages English, Math, and Reading.
  • Ranges, not guesses: every estimate keeps its uncertainty visible.

What to do with a number

  1. Compare it against the middle 50% of your target colleges.
  2. Find your two weakest skills and drill them.
  3. Retest in a week and watch the range move.

Frequently asked questions

Are these official score calculators?

No. They are unofficial estimates built from published curves and charts, clearly labeled as estimates.

Why show a range instead of one score?

Because curves vary by test date. A range is honest; a single number pretends to a precision the data does not have.

Can I compare my score to colleges?

Yes — the College screen shows each school's reported middle 50% and your position in it.

Does the calculator cost anything?

No, it is free with any practice set.
